FAQ

Is any of this financial advice?

No. It's T-shirts. It's jokes. Nothing on this site is investment, trading, or tax advice. Options can lose you more than you expect, faster than you expect. Act accordingly.

Can I buy a shirt right now?

Not yet. This is a preview. Prices are placeholders, there is no cart or checkout, and every button says Coming soon until a drop goes live.

When does the drop open?

Date TBA. When one is set, the countdown on the home page will show it. Join the drop list to hear first once sign-ups open.

What does “limited” mean here?

Designs drop in small runs. When a run closes, that design retires. Run details will be posted before launch.

Will there be restocks?

None are planned. That's the point of a drop.

How are shirts made and shipped?

Each shirt will be printed to order by a print-on-demand partner. Production times, shipping rates, and regions will be posted before launch.

Returns and exchanges?

The policy will be posted before anything goes on sale and will follow the print partner's terms for misprints and damaged items. Check the size guide first. It saves everyone pain.

Are you affiliated with a broker, exchange, campaign, or brand?

No. No broker, exchange, trading app, political campaign, or brand is involved with or endorses this.

Why is everything watermarked “PREVIEW”?

Because nothing is final or on sale yet. Mockups are marked so nobody mistakes them for a finished product.

What does 18+ mean?

The Green Candle category is innuendo. It is adult humor and is marked 18+ wherever it appears.
